# Everything in Iris's Overrides, Tweaks, Emote wheel and armor (2.2.0)

Keys (rebind under Controls): **K** skins, **J** settings, **C** zoom (hold), **G** fullbright, **F9** clean screenshot,
**F8** freecam, **F7** light overlay, **M** full map, **V** add waypoint, **[ ]** minimap zoom, **R** sort,
**B** (hold) emote wheel, **N** emote menu, **Y** save the newest shared waypoint.
Unbound by default: send my position, chat search, cycle cave map, toggle radar, villager reroller.

## Skins and players
- Change any player's skin on YOUR screen only (3D preview, PNG drop-in, download by username, per-player model, "restore last").
- Per-player chat name color, message color and nametag color (solid, rainbow, color cycle, any hex).
- AFK detection with tags over heads, in lists and in the inspect overlay.
- Crosshair inspect: health, held / worn items and enchantments of the player you look at.
- Reveal invisible players, and join / leave pop-ups.

## HUD (everything movable, resizable, themeable)
- Armor and held items (row or column, durability text, flashing low warning), effect timers (draining green-to-red bar, flash when low).
- Coordinates, direction, FPS, ping, light level; day counter and ticking clock; movable subtitles; keystrokes and CPS.
- Minimap, damage numbers, hotbar cooldown overlay with seconds left and a ready flash, AFK list, freecam indicator.
- HUD layout editor: drag to move, scroll to resize, right-click to reset, snapping.

## Map and navigation
- Live minimap (rotating or north-up, 4 zoom levels, players, mobs, waypoints) and a full pan / zoom map that remembers explored areas per world.
- Waypoints (named, recolored, in-world labels with distance), automatic death point.
- Waypoint sharing with friends through chat (friends list, inbox, send to chat or one friend, optional auto-save).
- Cave map (off / auto / always) and an entity radar with category filters, range and height limit.
- Pause-menu panel: day, exact time, sunset / next day countdowns, playtime (world, session, all worlds), real clock.

## Chat
- Custom colors for names, messages, join / leave / advancement / death lines, rainbow and cycling modes.
- 16 pixel emotes (:poggies: and more), text faces, 24 armor-icon emotes (:Dchest: :LLeg: ...).
- Chat heads, speech bubbles above players, text-to-speech through the game narrator.
- Timestamps, a sound and highlight when someone says your name, and searchable chat history.

## Combat and visuals
- Damage numbers, custom hit-flash color, 6 crosshair styles with hit marker and red-on-target.
- Freecam (speed, scroll, sprint boost, blocks actions, exits when hurt), light-level overlay, fullbright, instant zoom.
- Low fire (flames only at the bottom of the screen), clean screenshot (HUD hidden, name tags stay).

## Tools
- Chest, barrel, shulker, hopper, dispenser and inventory sorter (merge + sort by ID) with on-screen Sort / Stash / Same buttons and optional auto-sort when opened.
- Villager trade reroller: picks an enchantment, minimum level and maximum price and rerolls until it appears, with the Trade Cycling mod's key or by breaking the job block.
- Villager trades panel: look at a villager to see its remembered enchanted book trades.
- Weapon Master: hotbar swords, tools, trident, bows, shield and banner drawn on your character, shared with other players who have the mod.

## Emote Wheel and armor (nested mod)
- 310+ emotes (greetings, poses, dances, actions, moods, silly, anime, cat and maid) on a 3-page radial wheel with favorites, search, recents, random, replay and a live preview.
- Walk-while-emoting, particle effects (off / light / full) and 3D jutsu props.
- Cat / fox / bunny / bear ears, tails and a pet on your head with custom hex colors; Hinata and maid armor outfits (rename armor in an anvil).
- Shadow clones and the Cat_Girl skin swap; other players see your emotes if the server and they have the mod.

## Armor and mobs
- Maid and Hinata armor textures can each be switched off; armor can be hidden on you, on others or on everyone.
- No cat ears or tails on mobs (piglins, endermen ...); baby mobs keep their own arm size.

## Profiles and cosmetics
- Settings profiles: save and load whole setting sets, link one to a server or world for automatic switching.
- Capes and wings: built-in presets (shared with other mod users) and your own PNGs (only you see those).

## Theme and settings
- One settings screen with 9 tabs; presets (including Cherry Blossom and Pink Night), custom accent / background colors and opacity.
- The emote menu, wheel, settings and clone panel use the same theme and follow it live.
